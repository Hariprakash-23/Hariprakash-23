## 🚀 Projects

### 🏛️ [Govt Scheme AI — RAG Chatbot for Government Schemes & Eligibility](https://github.com/Hariprakash-23)
*Jun 2026 – Jul 2026*

An AI-powered government-service assistant that solves a real accessibility problem: citizens often struggle to find accurate, consolidated information about government schemes, eligibility criteria, required documents, and application procedures.

- Built an **end-to-end RAG pipeline**: extracts content from government PDFs using `pypdf`, chunks and embeds documents with **Sentence Transformers (all-MiniLM-L6-v2)**, retrieves relevant context through **ChromaDB semantic search**, and generates natural-language answers using the **Groq LLM**.
- Delivered as a conversational assistant through a **Flask REST API** backend paired with a **Streamlit** frontend.
- Integrated **scheme extraction** and **CSC (Common Service Center) lookup** so users get both the information and a path to act on it.

**Tech:** `Python` `Flask` `RAG` `pypdf` `Streamlit` `Groq` `ChromaDB` `Embeddings`

### 🎓 [Campus360 — Event-Driven Academic Management Platform](https://github.com/Hariprakash-23/SMS)
*Jan 2026 – Mar 2026*

A full academic management platform built to cut down the manual, paperwork-heavy workflows common in college administration.

- Architected a **Django** platform with **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)** dashboards for students and teachers, covering authentication, student registration, attendance tracking, and grade management.
- Designed a **cloud-native, event-driven backend**: **Amazon EventBridge** routes events, **AWS Lambda** runs serverless workflow logic, **Amazon SNS** handles automated notifications, and **Amazon S3** provides secure document storage.
- **Containerized with Docker** and shipped through a **CI/CD pipeline**, cutting deployment time from **15 minutes to under 3 minutes**.

**Tech:** `Django` `HTML` `CSS` `Amazon EventBridge` `AWS SNS` `AWS Lambda` `Boto3` `Docker` `Render`

### 🧠 [AI-Powered Quiz Generation Engine](https://github.com/Hariprakash-23)
*Jun 2025 – Jul 2025*

An adaptive assessment engine designed to replace static, one-size-fits-all quizzes with something that actually responds to how a student is doing.

- Built with **Python and Flask**, using a **Greedy algorithm** to analyze topic-wise performance and dynamically prioritize the next question shown.
- Adjusts **quiz difficulty in real time** based on ongoing performance and learning progress.
- Surfaces **personalized feedback**, helping identify knowledge gaps as they emerge rather than after the fact.

**Tech:** `Python` `Flask` `Greedy Algorithm` `HTML` `CSS`
